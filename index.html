07 · FAQ

% man opm

what is opm?

an onchain prediction terminal. it shows the world's live questions with real odds, lets you bet on them with SOL from your own wallet, and lets you launch a pump.fun coin that takes a side on any of them.

where do the odds come from?

from Polymarket, the biggest prediction market, read live in your browser. a price is what traders pay right now: 62¢ means the market thinks 62%.

how does a bet work?

opm finds the same Polymarket market on Solana through Jupiter, swaps your SOL to USDC, then opens the order on your side. your wallet signs both steps and the shares sit in your wallet. the minimum is about $5.

does opm hold my money?

no. bets and launches are transactions your own wallet signs. opm never holds your SOL or your keys.

what does it mean that a coin takes a side?

a coin launched here is born on one question and one side. the plan is for its creator fees to back that side and for winnings to go to its holders. that engine isn't switched on yet.

which wallets work?

Phantom, Solflare and other Solana wallets in your browser. every bet and launch shows up in your wallet first, and nothing happens until you sign.

can I lose?

yes. if your side loses, the shares pay nothing. never bet more than you can lose.
